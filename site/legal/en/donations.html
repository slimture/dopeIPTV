Voluntary – the app is and stays free

Donating to dopeIPTV is entirely voluntary. The app is free and will stay free, with or without your donation: no ads, no paid version, and nothing in the app is held back from anyone who does not donate – apart from the small thank-yous described below.

A donation is a gift to Solecraft AB, the company that makes dopeIPTV. Solecraft AB is a company, not a charity.

What the money goes to

Donations help pay for what it costs to keep dopeIPTV going:

  • developing the app;
  • the website and the servers that carry the downloads, the updates and the app's services;
  • Apple's developer programme, which lets the Mac version be signed and notarised so that it opens without warnings.

How a donation works

  • Press Donate on dopeiptv.org, choose an amount in your own currency, and pay by card or in another way our payment provider offers, such as Apple Pay or Google Pay if your device has it.
  • The payment is handled by our payment provider, [PAYMENT PROVIDER]. Your card details go straight to the provider; we never see them.
  • Each donation is a single payment – nothing is charged again.
  • You get a receipt by e-mail.
  • Would you like to give a larger amount, for example by bank transfer? Write to info@solecraft.se.

The supporter key – a thank-you, not a purchase

After a donation you get a supporter key: a short text that starts with DS1-. Paste it into the app under dopeIPTV → Supporter key…, or press Open in dopeIPTV in the thank-you. It brings a few small thank-yous:

  • a supporter mark – on the heart and in About dopeIPTV – and the app stops asking you to donate;
  • two supporter themes and a supporter icon for the app;
  • settings sync between your computers, end-to-end encrypted;
  • feedback you send from the app is marked as coming from a supporter and read first.

The key is not a purchase and not a licence: everything else in the app works the same without it. The thank-yous may develop along with the app.

The app checks the key itself, offline – there is no account and no sign-in. The key contains no personal data: only a version, the date of the donation, a number made from the payment and our signature. Nothing in it says who you are. dopeIPTV is open source, so anyone who builds the app themselves can change the check: in our builds the key is a thank-you, not a lock.

Getting your key again

  • The key is shown in the thank-you as soon as the payment has gone through, and your browser remembers it.
  • The receipt contains a link that shows your key again, whenever you like.
  • Lost both? Write to info@solecraft.se with the date and amount of your donation (or forward the receipt), and we will make the same key for you again. If you gave in another way, for example by bank transfer, we make a key for you by hand.

Changed your mind? Your money back

Write to info@solecraft.se within 14 days of your donation and we will pay back the whole amount – you do not need to explain why. Tell us the date and amount and the e-mail address on the receipt. The money goes back through the payment provider to the card or account you paid with, and it can take a few days before it shows.

As a consumer you generally have a 14-day right of withdrawal when you buy something at a distance, under the Swedish Distance Contracts Act (lag (2005:59) om distansavtal och avtal utanför affärslokaler). For digital content that is delivered at once – such as the supporter key – the right of withdrawal can end when the delivery starts, if you have agreed to that. We do not make use of that: if you ask within 14 days, you get your donation back anyway. Has it been longer? Write to us all the same, and we will look at it kindly.

Because the app checks the key itself, we cannot switch it off from here. After a refund, please remove it in the app (dopeIPTV → Supporter key… → Remove key).

Personal data

How donations and supporter keys are handled is described in the privacy policy. In short: card details go only to the payment provider, and the supporter key holds nothing about you.
