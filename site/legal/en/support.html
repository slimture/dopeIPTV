dopeIPTV is free, open-source software made by Solecraft AB. If you use it and like it, you can say thanks with a tip for the app – once, any amount you choose. It is entirely voluntary: the app is the same for everyone, and nothing is given for a tip.

What a tip is

A tip is a voluntary thank-you for software you already have and use. You get nothing for it – no feature, no other perk: the app is exactly the same with or without a tip.

Tips go to Solecraft AB, the company that makes dopeIPTV. Solecraft AB is a company, not a charity: a tip is not a gift to a charitable cause.

dopeIPTV is only an app – a player. It does not contain, sell, provide or link to any channels, playlists, streams or other content. You add playlists and services you already have yourself. A tip is a thank-you for the software, never payment for content.

How paying works
  • Press Give a tip, choose the amount yourself and pay once – by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, in a payment form on this site that Stripe provides. Nothing is charged again.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., Ireland). Your card details go straight to Stripe in Stripe's form; we never see them.
  • Stripe sends you a receipt by e-mail. Your card statement shows SOLECRAFT DOPEIPTV as the payee.
  • Would you rather tip another way, for example by bank transfer, or a larger amount? Write to info@solecraft.se.

Monthly support from before

Until 22 November 2026 you could also support the app every month. It is not offered any longer: monthly support that was still running has been cancelled by us, and nothing more is charged. If anything was charged after 22 November 2026, write to info@solecraft.se and you get it back.

Changed your mind? Your money back

Write to info@solecraft.se within 14 days of your tip and we pay back the whole amount. You do not need to explain why. Tell us the date and amount and the e-mail address on the receipt. The money goes back through Stripe to the card or account you paid with, and it can take a few days before it shows. Has it been longer? Write to us all the same, and we will look at it kindly.

Something wrong with a payment?

A payment you do not recognise or a wrong amount: write to us first at info@solecraft.se. We answer within a few working days and put it right, normally with a refund – faster than a dispute through your bank.

You can always dispute a card payment with your bank. We then answer the bank, through Stripe, with what we know about the payment. If we cannot agree, you can turn to the Swedish National Board for Consumer Disputes (Allmänna reklamationsnämnden, arn.se).

Personal data

How tips are handled is described in the privacy policy. In short: card details go only to Stripe, and we keep only what the bookkeeping needs.
