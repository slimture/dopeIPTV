dopeIPTV is free, open-source software made by Solecraft AB. If you use it and like it, you can say thanks with a tip – once, or every month. It is entirely voluntary: the app stays free for everyone, and nothing is sold in return.

What a tip is

A tip is a voluntary thank-you for software you already use. You get nothing for it that others do not get – the app is the same with or without a tip – apart from the small thank-yous described below.

Tips go to Solecraft AB, the company that makes dopeIPTV. Solecraft AB is a company, not a charity: a tip is not a gift to a charitable cause.

dopeIPTV is only an app – a player. It does not contain, sell, provide or link to any channels, playlists, streams or other content. You add playlists and services you already have yourself. A tip is support for the software, never payment for content.

What the money goes to
  • developing the app;
  • the website and the servers that carry the downloads, the updates and the app's services;
  • Apple's developer programme, which lets the Mac version be signed and notarised so that it opens without warnings.

How paying works
  • A one-time tip: press Give a tip, choose the amount yourself and pay once – by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, in a payment form on this site that Stripe provides. Nothing is charged again.
  • Monthly support: press Support monthly, which takes you to Stripe's own payment page. The same amount is charged once a month until you cancel.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., Ireland). Whether in Stripe's form on our page or on Stripe's own page, your card details go straight to Stripe; we never see them. You pay by card or another way Stripe offers where you live.
  • Stripe sends you a receipt by e-mail. Your card statement shows SOLECRAFT DOPEIPTV as the payee.
  • You can also support the project through GitHub Sponsors. GitHub then handles the payment, under GitHub's terms.
  • Would you rather give another way, for example by bank transfer? Write to info@solecraft.se.

Cancelling monthly support

You can cancel whenever you like, with no notice period. Use Manage or cancel your monthly support – a link comes to your e-mail – or the link in Stripe's e-mail. You can also write to info@solecraft.se and we cancel it for you. Nothing more is charged after you cancel.

The supporter key – a thank-you, not a purchase

After a tip, or the first payment of monthly support, you get a supporter key: a short text that starts with DS1-. Paste it into the app under Help → Supporter key… (on a Mac dopeIPTV → Supporter key…), or press Open in dopeIPTV where the key is shown. It brings a few small thank-yous:

  • a supporter mark – on the heart and in About dopeIPTV – and the app stops asking for support;
  • two supporter themes and a supporter icon for the app;
  • feedback you send from the app is marked as coming from a supporter and read first.

The key is not a purchase and not a licence: everything else in the app works the same without it – settings sync between your computers, for example, is for everyone. The thank-yous may develop along with the app.

The app checks the key itself, offline – there is no account and no sign-in. The key contains no personal data: only a version, the date of the payment, a number made from the payment and our signature. Nothing in it says who you are. dopeIPTV is open source, so anyone who builds the app themselves can change the check: in our builds the key is a thank-you, not a lock.

Getting your key again
  • The key is shown as soon as the payment has gone through – after a tip in the thank-you, after monthly support on the support page – and your browser remembers it: open the support page again in the same browser to see it. A tip's receipt also has a link that shows its key again.
  • Lost it? Write to info@solecraft.se with the date and amount (or forward Stripe's receipt), and we make the same key for you again. If you gave another way, we make a key for you by hand.
  • A key from before November 2026 keeps working. Lost one of those? Write to us and we make you a new one.

Changed your mind? Your money back

Write to info@solecraft.se within 14 days of a payment – a tip or a month of support – and we pay back the whole amount. You do not need to explain why. Tell us the date and amount and the e-mail address on the receipt. The money goes back through Stripe to the card or account you paid with, and it can take a few days before it shows.

As a consumer you generally have a 14-day right of withdrawal when you buy something at a distance, under the Swedish Distance Contracts Act (lag (2005:59) om distansavtal och avtal utanför affärslokaler). For digital content that is delivered at once – such as the supporter key – the right of withdrawal can end when the delivery starts, if you have agreed to that. We do not make use of that: if you ask within 14 days, you get your money back anyway. Has it been longer? Write to us all the same, and we will look at it kindly.

Because the app checks the key itself, we cannot switch it off from here. After a refund, please remove it in the app (Help → Supporter key… → Remove key; on a Mac dopeIPTV → Supporter key… → Remove key).

Something wrong with a payment?

A payment you do not recognise, a wrong amount, a charge after you cancelled: write to us first at info@solecraft.se. We answer within a few working days and put it right, normally with a refund – faster than a dispute through your bank.

You can always dispute a card payment with your bank. We then answer the bank, through Stripe, with what we know about the payment. If we cannot agree, you can turn to the Swedish National Board for Consumer Disputes (Allmänna reklamationsnämnden, arn.se).

Personal data

How payments and supporter keys are handled is described in the privacy policy. In short: card details go only to Stripe, and the supporter key holds nothing about you.
