1. What dopeIPTV is

dopeIPTV is a free media player for computers (Linux, macOS and Windows). It plays streams from services you add yourself – for example a subscription you have through Xtream Codes, an M3U playlist, a Stalker portal or a box on your home network – and your own files.

dopeIPTV does not contain, sell, provide, host or link to any TV channels, playlists, streams, films, series or other content. Solecraft AB is not an IPTV provider, does not sell or arrange subscriptions and has no connection to any provider. The only exception is the demo channels: a few public test videos – such as Big Buck Bunny, Sintel and Tears of Steel – that their publishers, Mux, Apple and Bitmovin, make available for testing video players, so that you can try the app before you add a service of your own.

Posters, descriptions and subtitles that the app can show come from outside services (TMDB and OpenSubtitles) when you use those features.
