Who makes dopeIPTV

dopeIPTV is made by Solecraft AB, a Swedish limited company. The app is free and open source (GPL-3.0-or-later), and its source code is public on GitHub.

What dopeIPTV is

A free media player for Linux, macOS and Windows. It plays streams from the services you add – IPTV subscriptions through Xtream Codes, M3U playlists, Stalker portals and boxes on your home network – and your own video and music files. It has a programme guide, can rewind live TV, record, show several channels at once and send the picture to a TV.

Solecraft AB is not an IPTV provider and has no connection to any. We do not sell, arrange or recommend subscriptions, and the app comes with no channels – only a few public test videos to try it with.
