# Stills for the website's pictures

`tools/site_screenshots.py` shows these in the players of the pictures on
the front page of iptv.dope.rs. They are NASA photographs, in the public
domain (works of the U.S. federal government), cut to 16:9:

| File | Photograph |
|---|---|
| `orbit.jpg` | *The Blue Marble* - Earth seen from Apollo 17, 1972 |
| `cosmos.jpg` | Saturn during equinox - Cassini, 2009 |
| `planet.jpg` | Jupiter and its shrunken Great Red Spot - Hubble, 2014 |
| `launch.jpg` | Space Shuttle Columbia launching |

All from Wikimedia Commons, under those titles. Any other picture put here
must be as free to use: the website shows it to everyone.
